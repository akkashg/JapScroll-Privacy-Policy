Privacy Policy

Naam Jap Scroll · Last updated 24 October 2026

Naam Jap Scroll helps you spend less time scrolling by interrupting an app you have asked it to watch, and offering a short naam jap or meditation instead.

The app works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Nothing you chant, nothing you count and nothing you look at is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never transmitted, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

WhatWhy
The naam you chose, and your break settingsSo the app behaves the way you set it up
Which apps you asked to be watched, and their limitsTo know when to interrupt
How long those apps have been used todayTo apply the limits you set
Your jap and meditation history, and daily totalsTo show your own progress

Which app is in front

To know when to interrupt, the app needs to see which app is currently open. It uses Android's Usage access permission, which you grant yourself in system settings and can withdraw at any time.

This is read moment to moment and used only to decide whether to show you a break. The app records how long a watched app has been used, on your device. It does not record what you did inside that app, what you looked at, what you typed, or anything on your screen — and it has no technical means to do so.

What leaves your device

One thing, and only if you allow it: crash reports.

If the app breaks, a report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the fault can be fixed. A report contains the technical details of the crash, your device model, operating system version, and an identifier Crashlytics generates for the installation. It does not contain your naam, your counts, your history, or the names of the apps you watch.

You can switch this off. Open Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports. Switched off, collection stops at the source — nothing is gathered and held back; nothing is gathered at all.

Crashlytics is provided by Google. Their handling of that data is covered by the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app deliberately does not do

  • No analytics. Nothing records which screens you open, which buttons you press, or how you use the app. Google Analytics is switched off in the project, not merely unused.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No microphone. The app once counted naam by listening; that was removed and the recording permission with it.
  • No accessibility service. Also removed. The app cannot read your screen.
  • No contacts, location, photos, files or messages.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, for any purpose.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, of any age.

Your data, and how to remove it

Because everything is held on your own device, you are in control of it:

  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → Naam Jap Scroll → Storage → Clear data. This removes your history, settings and timers permanently.
  • Stop crash reports: switch them off in the app's Settings.
  • Withdraw a permission: any of the above, in Android's own settings, at any time.

There is no account to close and no copy of your data elsewhere to request or erase.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed with it.
